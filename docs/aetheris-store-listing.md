## Long Summary (1500 character limit)

Aetheris is a companion space for astrology, tarot, and meditation — built for practitioners who want their practice to feel considered, not gimmicky.

Inside a calm dual-pane HUD, you can:

**Astrology** — Track planetary alignments and explore your natal chart in an interface designed to make the data legible, not overwhelming.

**A Window Into Space** — Look through a stereographic window rendering a live solar system view, so the movement of the planets feels present rather than abstract.

**Tarot** — Draw cards for reflection and guidance, presented with the respect the practice deserves.

**Meditation & Mantra** — Guided meditation sessions paired with mantras, along with context on what they mean and why they're used, for newcomers and experienced practitioners alike.

**Grimoire & Journal** — A private, local journal space to record readings, reflections, and your own notes over time.

Aetheris was built by developers who spent real time with the esoteric and spiritual communities it serves, aiming for a tool that feels like it was made with care rather than mass-produced. Whether you're deep into your practice or just starting to explore astrology and tarot, Aetheris gives you a quiet space to return to.

More features are planned as the app grows beyond its initial release.

## Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy for Aetheris**
*Last updated: 9/2/2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how Aetheris ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use our application on Meta Quest devices.

**Information We Collect**

Aetheris is designed to keep your personal reflections private:

- **Journal entries, tarot readings, and personal notes** are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or have access to this content.
- **Account and purchase information** (such as your Meta account identifier and purchase history) is handled by Meta's platform in accordance with Meta's own privacy policy, as required for app distribution and payment processing on the Meta Quest Store.

**Data Storage**

All journal, chart, and reading data created within Aetheris is stored locally on your headset. Uninstalling the app will remove this data. We recommend backing up any entries you want to keep before uninstalling.

**Permissions**

Aetheris requests only the device permissions necessary for core functionality (e.g., local storage access for journal entries). We do not access camera, microphone, or location data beyond what the Meta Quest OS itself requires for standard operation.

**Children's Privacy**

Aetheris is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

**Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy as Aetheris evolves. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.
